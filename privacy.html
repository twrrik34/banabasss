Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 20, 2026

BANABASSS is a simple online clicking game with an optional global leaderboard. This page explains what information the site may process.

Information we process

If you sign in with Google, authentication is handled by Supabase Auth and Google. BANABASSS may store a user identifier, username, click count, and nickname-change information needed to operate the leaderboard.

If you use Guest mode, your clicks are kept only for the current browser session and are not added to the global leaderboard.

How information is used

Information is used to provide sign-in, save your score, display the leaderboard, prevent inconsistent nickname changes, and maintain the service.

Third parties

The site uses Supabase for authentication and database functionality. Google may process information when you choose Google sign-in. If advertising is enabled in the future, an advertising provider may use cookies or similar technologies according to its own policies.

Contact

If you have a privacy question, please contact the site owner through the project's public GitHub repository.

Changes

This policy may be updated when the site adds features such as advertising or new account functionality.
