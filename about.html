What is BANABASSS?

BANABASSS is a lightweight global clicking game. The idea is intentionally simple: choose a nickname, press the button, build your score, and see how you compare with other players.

CLICKCOMPETECLIMB

How it works

Players can enter with Google or play temporarily as a guest. Signed-in players can save their click count and appear on the global leaderboard. Guest clicks are local to the current session.

Why a leaderboard?

The leaderboard turns a one-button game into a small competition. Every score has a place, and the goal is to keep climbing.

Built for the web

BANABASSS is designed to work on phones and computers, with a responsive interface and a lightweight front end. Authentication and persistent scores are powered by Supabase.
